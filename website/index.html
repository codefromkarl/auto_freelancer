Your device is the execution environment.

Model requests go from your computer to the HTTPS model endpoint you select. They are not relayed through BidMaster's servers. Cloud models still receive the brief and background needed for your request.

  • Model keys and platform tokens are not shared with the publisher.
  • Credential storage uses the OS vault, with no plaintext fallback.
  • Diagnostics omit credentials, project contents and raw logs.
  • Upgrades and license expiry preserve your projects and submission records.

Windows and macOS, with separate native verification.

Public downloads are enabled only after each native build, credential-vault check and production signing has passed. Preview artifacts are not production releases.

Checking release status…

Invitation-only preview

If the publisher shared a preview build with you, send them the installation ID shown in the app to request a signed trial. Model API usage is billed separately by your model provider.

Paid checkout is not enabled while the merchant, commercial terms and native release verification are being finalized.

Automation needs permission, not just a token.

The core workbench can analyze imported briefs without connecting to a freelance platform. Platform automation requires separate verified written platform permission, your own account authorization and an explicit execution choice. Buying a software license does not grant that permission.

Local background work requires your computer to stay awake and connected. BidMaster does not accept awards, move funds or send customer messages on your behalf.
