Describe your real capabilities

Start with one complete brief

Keep execution visible

Activity shows queued, running, completed, failed and interrupted tasks. Pause prevents new work while current requests finish safely. Quit safely when finished; closing the browser alone does not stop the local app. The computer must remain awake and connected for background work.

Platform access and updates

Platform automation stays locked unless written platform permission has been verified separately and your own account is connected. Keep signed release information and use the verified installer for your OS. Never replace a newer ledger with an older backup without reconciliation.
